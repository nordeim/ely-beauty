**Lessons for the next cycle:** the run-103 lesson ("a shape has TWO sides") has a third face the audits keep finding one run late: a grammar's INVARIANT COMMENT can outlive the grammar's actual behavior. When a grammar ships with an exclusion invariant (a lookahead, a boundary rule), the pin that proves it must feed the grammar the EXCLUDED shape and assert both the clean exclusion AND the un-mangled match text — an invariant nobody probes is a claim nobody checked; and backtracking is a standing witness that a regex's documented intent and its operational semantics are two different artifacts, to be reconciled by probe, not by comment.
